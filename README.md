# FC Concordia - Friday Night Open Play Registration

Official registration portal for FC Concordia's weekly football open plays.

## About the Project
This website streamlines the registration and team assignment process for our weekly Friday open play sessions. 

### Key Features
- **Online Sign-Up:** Attendees can pay via GCash QR code, upload their proof of payment, and select their preferred team.
- **Live Roster:** Displays real-time team lists and available player slots (9 slots per team across 4 teams).
- **Admin Dashboard:** Password-protected dashboard for organizers to manage attendee lists, verify payments, and format GCash announcements for Messenger.

## Open Play Details
* **When:** Every Friday | 8:00 PM – 10:00 PM
* **Where:** Turf BGC, Taguig
* **Fee:** ₱300 per player
* **Format:** 4 Teams, 9 Players per Team
